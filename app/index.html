Culture link

1Pi 5 samples SHT45 air + DS18B20 leaf temperature, GNSS position
2sensor vector sent over Wi-Fi / LTE to the culture service (HTTPS / WebSocket)
3service stimulates the MEA sensory region, reads DEPLOY / HOLD spike counts
4DEPLOY: drive to the row, extend L16 actuator, unroll AG-30 cover
5link down: fall back to threshold rule (deploy if air < +1 °C)
